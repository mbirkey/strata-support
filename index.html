Strata

A multi-track recorder for iPhone and iPad. Record with unlimited tracks, shape each one with effects, and export your mix — projects live in iCloud Drive.

Get help or report a problem

Support happens through GitHub Issues — bug reports, questions, and feature requests all welcome. No account email required to browse existing answers.

Quick answers

Where are my projects? In iCloud Drive under “Strata” (Files app), synced to all your devices. Signed out of iCloud? They stay on-device.

Monitoring is silent? Live input monitoring needs wired or USB headphones — it stays off on the speaker (feedback) and Bluetooth (latency).

USB microphones? Plug one in and Strata switches to it automatically; unplug to return to the built-in mic.

Strata is free. The app collects no data, has no analytics, and makes no network requests of its own — your recordings stay in your iCloud. Read the full privacy policy.
